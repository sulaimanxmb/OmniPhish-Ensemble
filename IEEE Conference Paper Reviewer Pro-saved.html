Summary Conclusion

The paper offers a technically valid engineering contribution regarding structural tokenization and LoRA-fine-tuned CodeBERT usage. However, the lack of rigorous statistical validation, combined with a biased dataset that distorts real-world security performance, necessitates significant revision before it can be considered for publication. The "Weak Reject" status is driven by the need for concrete evidence regarding hardware viability and performance comparison against current SOTA.
